# Finance & Capital Systems Package

A modular collection of **Espanso** triggers and AI prompt engines for personal CFO budgeting, wealth architecture, equity research, cash flow optimization, and asymmetric leverage.

---

## 💼 Personal CFO Series

| Trigger | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `:cfo-audit` | **Full Money & Balance Sheet Audit** | Complete CFO assessment of income, expenses, debts, savings, and investments to find leaks. |
| `:cfo-wealth` | **Comprehensive Wealth Roadmap** | Step-by-step financial trajectory builder for compounding net worth and long-term security. |
| `:cfo-cashflow` | **Cash Flow & Automation Optimizer** | Assigns every monthly dollar a purpose, cutting waste and automating recurring allocations. |
| `:cfo-leaks` | **Money Leaks & Stealth Expense Ranker** | Uncovers hidden subscriptions and lifestyle creep, ranked by annual cost and minimal friction. |
| `:cfo-debt` | **Debt Destroyer & Payoff Strategy** | Mathematically optimal debt repayment plan (Avalanche vs Snowball) customized to cashflow. |
