# Career & Professional Trajectory Package

A modular collection of **Espanso** triggers and interactive AI prompt templates designed for senior engineering positioning, resume engineering, ATS optimization, and executive interview preparation.

---

## 🎯 Recruiter & Resume Review Series

| Trigger | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `:res-attention` | **The Recruiter Attention Test** | Evaluates 10-second first impressions, visual scan hierarchy, and initial credibility. |
| `:res-mindset` | **The Recruiter Mindset** | Assesses resume competitiveness against hundreds of candidates for senior roles. |
| `:res-ats` | **The ATS Visibility Engine** | Extracts missing technical keywords from target JDs and naturally integrates competencies. |
| `:res-impact` | **The Impact Statement Rebuilder** | Converts passive task descriptions into metric-driven, quantified business achievements. |
| `:res-market` | **The Market Positioning Rewrite** | Aligns tone, summary, and skills with the communication culture of top-tier firms. |
| `:res-executive` | **Executive Resume Refinement** | Final-pass deep audit eliminating corporate fluff and maximizing executive authority. |

## 📑 Evidentiary Resume & ATS Optimization Series (Ramonov & Robert Half 2026)

Built on the methodologies of Sabrina Ramonov (*"How to Make Your Resume Un-Rejectable"*), MIT career frameworks, and Robert Half's *2026 Resume Writing & ATS Screening Standards*.

| Trigger | Label & Concept | Method & Focus |
|---|---|---|
| `:p_factbank` | **Fact Bank & Master Inventory** | Socratic biographical AI interview building a permanent, verified career fact bank (Ramonov & Robert Half Master Document). |
| `:p_proofmatch` | **Job Match & Proof Hierarchy** | Ranks top 5 requirements and crafts evidence-backed bullets with Bullet 1 (top required proof) and Bullet 2 (best metric) (MIT & Ramonov). |
| `:p_recruiter` | **Recruiter Fit Stress Test** | Rigorous screen for "3 strong reasons & 3 doubts", ATS plain-text single-column formatting, and 3 high-pressure interview questions. |
| `:p_pivot` | **Transferable Skills & Pivot Bridge** | Maps cross-functional competencies and creates an ATS "Areas of Expertise" block for career transitions (Robert Half 2026). |
| `:p_fluffcheck` | **AI Fluff & ATS Screening Defense** | Audits draft against AI hallucinations, robotic buzzwords, and ATS parsing traps (Robert Half & Ramonov). |
| `:p_aiskills` | **AI Skills Chat History Mining** | Scans 60 days of workspace conversations to isolate concrete AI tools, agent workflows, and prompt engineering bullets. |
| `:p_atsapply` | **ATS Application Form Assist** | Guides browser AI agents through company careers pages with a mandatory human safety stop before submission. |
